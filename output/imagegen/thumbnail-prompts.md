## Swipe Fotos — referencia

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: square portfolio project thumbnail, style exploration 1 of 4.
Primary request: Create ONE standalone polished thumbnail for Swipe Fotos, an iOS app for organizing a camera roll by swiping photos to keep or delete, month by month. Visually communicate photo curation and a swipe deck.
Style/medium: Premium tactile 3D clay and frosted glass illustration. Floating softly rounded photographic cards, subtle translucent swipe arcs, a tiny keep check and discard cross integrated into the composition. Soft diffuse studio light, realistic contact shadows, pearl white background with pale rose and icy blue accents. Refined restrained playful 3D, no phone.
Subject: A cohesive stack of scenic travel photos (mountains, sea, quiet landscape), with a clear visual sense of a swipe decision.
Composition/framing: square 1:1 image; main subject occupies central 60 percent, weighted a little above center. Keep upper corners and bottom quarter calm and nearly empty for the existing portfolio category badge and title that will be overlaid later. Attractive at 280px wide. Full bleed background, no surrounding card frame.
Constraints: No words, no letters, no numbers, no project title, no watermarks, no branding. One image, no comparison sheet or grid. Avoid visual clutter. Reusable visual system suitable for a homogeneous set of nine software project thumbnails.
