# Yantissimo — thumbnail principal

Herramienta: image_gen integrada. Referencia: Swipe Fotos 3D aprobado.

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: flagship square portfolio thumbnail for Yantissimo Platform, larger hero card spanning two rows and two columns. This is the centerpiece of an existing homogeneous software portfolio.
Input image: STYLE REFERENCE ONLY, the approved Swipe Fotos thumbnail. Precisely match its sophisticated soft 3D product illustration, warm pearl white environment, rounded objects, frosted transparent glass ribbons, diffuse studio light, delicate realistic shadows and pastel colors. No landscape photos.
Primary request: A beautifully art-directed miniature connected ecosystem for an entire tire and wheel distribution business, covering FOUR coordinated software surfaces and its daily business operations. Give it the care, coherent perspective, subtle material richness, and composition of an award-winning enterprise product illustration. It must be immediately identifiable as an automotive tire business powered by connected software.
Composition and hierarchy:
- Central visual anchor, a gorgeous realistic black rubber automotive tire with finely modeled tread and a machined silver alloy wheel, angled three-quarter view, resting in front of a large floating pearl-white desktop ERP screen. This tire is the strongest dark contrast and recognizable business symbol.
- Behind the tire, the largest screen shows a clean abstract operational dashboard: small violet/blue bar chart and growth line, an orderly sales/order grid with colored status chips, and a small monthly scheduling grid. Graphical marks only, no text or numbers.
- On the RIGHT a smaller floating modern smartphone in pearl-silver, with a dark rounded screen inset displaying a tire product image, small appointment card with calendar icon, a loyalty heart and tiny notification badge. Represents the native customer app.
- On the LEFT a smaller elegant rounded storefront browser panel with a restrained pastel amber awning detail, two beautiful tire product thumbnails and a small white car silhouette above three blank selection pills, representing shopping and vehicle fitment search.
- At the rear center, partly visible behind the primary ERP screen, a refined frosted-blue database cylinder on a tiny two-layer pearl-white server plinth. It is the shared backend and single data source.
- A single elegant flowing translucent glass ribbon in pale sky blue and warm amber loops around the ensemble, visibly connecting desktop, mobile, storefront and server. A few tiny softly glowing dots convey real-time synchronization, not a technical circuit diagram.
Business operation details, carefully integrated as small physical props rather than an icon cloud:
- Lower left, a tiny pearl-white warehouse rack holding a few dark rubber tires, with two tasteful small cartons representing wholesale inventory and orders.
- Lower right, a tiny white car on a low minimalist service lift with a small silver wrench, conveying installation and workshop operations.
- Beside the desktop base, a neat pair of floating ivory invoice/quote sheets with simple line markings, a tiny grid spreadsheet tile feeding the backend, and a small frosted mint audit shield with a checkmark for reconciliation, warranties and quality control.
- A very small pastel lavender sparkle/chat tab attached to the ERP bezel suggests embedded AI assistance; two tiny subtle colored cursor shapes on the dashboard suggest collaboration.
Art direction: intelligent restraint. Main objects carry 80% of visual weight; micro details are discoveries on closer inspection. Do not give all objects equal emphasis. All objects inhabit ONE coherent floating vignette, no collage of disconnected icons. Use black only for rubber tires and small screen details; pearly ceramic, brushed silver, frosted blue glass and subtle warm amber accents elsewhere. Match the reference's pastel saturation and luminous white background. Realistic material detailing on tire tread, rim and glass, soft rounded construction on supporting props. No neon, no harsh metallic science-fiction look.
Framing: square 1:1. Main ecosystem fits comfortably within central 84% of width, upper 76% of height. Leave bottom 20% a calm near-white surface and gentle shadow for the portfolio title overlay. Leave top corners clear for external badges. Keep entire main tire and all three device silhouettes visible without clipping. View from modestly elevated three-quarter camera, consistent soft studio illumination.
Constraints: no written words, no letters, no numbers, no software vendor logos, no watermark, no captions, no surrounding card border, no infographic labels or connector arrowheads. Not a literal screenshot. ONE exceptional polished image, full bleed pearl background.
